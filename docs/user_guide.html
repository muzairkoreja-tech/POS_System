1. System Overview
What is this POS system and what can it do?

This is a complete Point of Sale and Business Management System built for retail supermarkets and stores. It handles everything from ringing up a sale at the counter to generating a Profit & Loss statement at the end of the month.


The system is organized into 7 main modules, each designed for a specific team in the business:


🛒 POS
Sales counter, receipts, shifts
📦 Inventory
Products, stock, QR codes
🚚 Procurement
Vendors, POs, deliveries
💰 Finance
Accounts, journal, ledger
👥 HR
Employees, payroll
📊 Reports
16 reports, Excel export
🤖 AI Analytics
Predictive forecasts

👤 User Roles & Permissions

Every person who logs in has a role. The role controls which parts of the system they can see and use.

RoleWho Uses ItAccess Level
AdminBusiness Owner / ManagerFull access to every screen and setting in the system.
CashierCounter StaffPOS terminal, shifts, returns. Cannot access finance or procurement.
Sales PersonSales TeamSales dashboard, customers, credit sales, reports.
Procurement ManagerBuying / Store ManagerFull procurement module, vendors, inventory, reports.
💡 TIP: Only an Admin can create or edit other user accounts. If someone cannot see a screen, check their role in System Users.

Login & Authentication
Screens: Login, Forgot Password, Reset Password
Login Login Page /login
The first page every user sees. Enter your username and password to access the system. The system will redirect you to the correct dashboard based on your role.
  1. Open your browser and go to the system URL (e.g. http://localhost:5000)
  2. Enter your Username and Password
  3. Click Sign In — you will be taken to your dashboard
  4. If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password?"
⚠️ After 3 failed login attempts the system will block the account. Contact your Admin to reset it.
Password Forgot / Reset Password /forgot-password
If you forget your password, the system can send a reset link to your registered email address.
  1. Click Forgot Password? on the login page
  2. Enter your registered email address and click Send
  3. Check your email inbox for a reset link (valid for 1 hour)
  4. Click the link, enter your new password, and confirm it

3. Point of Sale (POS)
The cashier counter — scan, sell, collect payment
Cashier POS Terminal /
The main cashier screen. This is where every sale happens. Scan or search for products, apply discounts, collect payment and print receipts.

How to Complete a Sale

  1. Start Shift — A shift must be open before selling. The system will prompt you if no shift is active.
  2. Add Products — Scan a barcode with a scanner or type the product name in the search box and press Enter.
  3. Adjust Quantities — Click the + / − buttons on each item or type the quantity directly.
  4. Apply Discounts — Use the cart discount field for a percentage off the total, or enter a promo code.
  5. Select Customer (optional) — Search for a registered customer to earn loyalty points.
  6. Choose Payment Method — Cash, Card, UBL, Meezan, EasyPaisa or split payment.
  7. Enter Amount Tendered — System calculates change automatically for cash payments.
  8. Click CHARGE — Receipt is generated. Inventory is updated instantly.
Payment MethodDescription
CashEnter amount tendered, system shows change due
CardDebit/Credit card payment (no change)
UBL / MeezanBank transfer payment
EasyPaisa / JazzCashMobile wallet payment
SplitCustomer pays with multiple methods (e.g. half cash, half card)
Credit / AccountSale recorded as owed — goes to Credit Sales AR
⚠️ You cannot sell a product that has zero stock. The system will block the sale and show an alert.
Cashier Shift Management /pos/shifts
Shifts track all sales made by a cashier during their working period. Each shift records the opening and closing float, total sales, and payment breakdown.
  1. Click Open Shift and enter the opening cash float amount
  2. Process sales during the shift as normal
  3. At end of shift, click Close Shift and enter the closing cash count
  4. System shows the expected vs actual cash — any difference is flagged
  5. Print the Z-Report for a complete shift summary
Cashier Sales Returns /pos/returns
Process a refund when a customer returns goods. The system reverses the sale and restores inventory.
  1. Search for the original sale by receipt number or date
  2. Select the items being returned and the quantity
  3. Enter the return reason (e.g. defective, wrong item, customer changed mind)
  4. Select refund method (cash or reverse to original payment)
  5. Click Process Return — stock is restored, refund is recorded
Admin / Sales Credit Sales (Accounts Receivable) /credit-sales
View and manage all sales that were made on credit (payment deferred). Track outstanding balances per customer and record payments when received.
💡 Always link credit sales to a registered customer so you can track who owes what. Walk-in credit sales should be avoided.
Cashier / Admin Customers & Loyalty /pos/customers
Manage your customer database. Registered customers earn loyalty points on every purchase and can redeem them for discounts on future sales.
FieldRequiredNotes
NameRequiredCustomer full name
PhoneRequiredUsed for lookup at the counter
EmailOptionalFor receipts and promotions
Loyalty PointsAutoEarned automatically on each purchase
Admin / Cashier Z-Report (End of Day Report) /z-report
The Z-Report is the official end-of-day report. It shows total sales, payment method breakdown, discounts given, taxes collected, number of transactions, and cashier performance for the selected shift or date. Print this for daily accounting records.

Admin QR Code Generator /procurement/qr
Generate and print QR codes for products. Customers can scan QR codes to view product details and pricing. Also useful for shelf labels. Select products and print a sheet of labels.

5. Procurement
Vendors, purchase orders, deliveries, invoices and payments

Manage your supplier database. Every vendor you buy from should be registered here before creating a purchase order.
FieldRequiredNotes

Phone / EmailOptionalFor ordering and communication
Payment TermsOptionale.g. Net 30, Net 60 — used for invoice aging
NTN / STRNOptionalTax numbers for FBR compliance
Procurement Purchase Requisitions (PR) /procurement/pr
A Purchase Requisition is an internal request to buy something. It starts the procurement process. A PR is raised by the store team and then approved by management to become a Purchase Order.
  1. Click New Requisition
  2. Select the vendor and add the products needed with quantities
  3. Submit the PR — it shows as Pending
  4. Manager approves it → it becomes a Purchase Order
💡 PRs create a paper trail. Never skip this step even for urgent purchases — it protects the business.
Procurement Purchase Orders (PO) /procurement/po
A Purchase Order is the official buying document sent to the vendor. It confirms the items, quantities and agreed price. The PO status tracks the delivery lifecycle.
StatusMeaning
DraftCreated but not yet sent to vendor
ApprovedManagement approved — sent to vendor
Partially ReceivedSome items arrived, waiting for the rest
ReceivedAll items delivered and checked in via Goods Receipt
CancelledOrder was cancelled
Procurement Goods Receipt (GR) /procurement/gr
When a delivery arrives at the store, record it here. The Goods Receipt confirms what was actually received against the Purchase Order. Stock is added to inventory automatically on GR creation.
  1. Select the Purchase Order the delivery is for
  2. Enter the actual quantities received per item (may differ from ordered)
  3. Note any damaged or missing items
  4. Click Save GR — stock is updated immediately
  5. A Vendor Invoice is generated automatically for billing
🔴 Only create a GR when goods are physically in the warehouse. Never GR before delivery — this inflates stock incorrectly.
Procurement Vendor Invoices /procurement/invoices
All invoices received from vendors. Invoices are auto-created from Goods Receipts. Track payment status, due dates and outstanding amounts here. This is your Accounts Payable record.
Procurement Vendor Payments /procurement/payments
Record payments made to vendors against their invoices. Select the invoice, enter the amount paid and payment method. The invoice status updates to Paid or Partial automatically.
Procurement Product Catalog /procurement/catalog
A vendor-linked product catalog for procurement. Link products to specific vendors with vendor pricing and lead times. Makes creating purchase orders faster as products are pre-associated with their suppliers.

6. Finance
Chart of accounts, journal entries, general ledger and financial overview
Admin Finance Dashboard /finance
A real-time financial summary showing total revenue, expenses, net profit, and key financial ratios. Includes charts for revenue trends, expense breakdown, and monthly profit. This is the first screen managers check for financial health.
Admin Chart of Accounts (COA) /finance/coa
The master list of all financial accounts used in the business (e.g. Cash, Sales Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, Payroll). The system comes with standard accounts pre-configured. Add custom accounts if needed.
Account TypeExamples
AssetsCash, Bank, Inventory, Receivables
LiabilitiesAccounts Payable, Loans
EquityOwner's Capital, Retained Earnings
RevenueSales Revenue, Other Income
ExpensesCost of Goods Sold, Payroll, Rent
Admin Journal Entries /finance/journal
View all financial journal entries. Sales, purchases and payroll are posted automatically. Manual journal entries can be made here for adjustments (e.g. depreciation, accruals, corrections).
💡 Every sale creates a journal entry automatically: Debit Cash, Credit Sales Revenue. You don't need to post these manually.
Admin General Ledger /finance/gl
A complete transaction-by-transaction record for each account. Select any account from the COA to see every debit and credit posted to it, with running balances. Used for month-end reconciliation.
Admin Payments /payments
Record and view all outgoing and incoming payments: vendor payments, utility bills, rent, salaries and other business expenses. Keeps cash flow records accurate.

10. FBR Integration
Federal Board of Revenue tax compliance
Admin Only FBR Dashboard /fbr
Manages FBR (Pakistan Federal Board of Revenue) tax integration. Tracks sales tax collected, generates FBR-compliant reports and manages NTN/STRN numbers for the business and its vendors. Required for businesses registered with FBR under the Point of Sale Integration scheme.
⚠️ FBR integration settings should only be changed by the Admin in consultation with your tax advisor. Incorrect settings can cause compliance issues.
