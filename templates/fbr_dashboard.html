📜 What FBR Integration Does

Every sale is reported to FBR in real-time as a fiscal invoice
Receipts display FBR invoice number + verifiable QR code
Sales tax collected is automatically tracked per transaction
GST returns are pre-populated from synced data
Customers get PKR 10 prize on winning lucky draw receipts
Full audit trail of all fiscal transmissions stored locally

⚠ Penalties for Non-Compliance

PKR 10,000 per day for operating POS without FBR integration (if turnover exceeds threshold)
Business seal by FBR inspectors for repeated non-compliance
Tax audit triggered automatically for unregistered businesses
Businesses with annual turnover > PKR 10M are required to integrate
Registered businesses get reduced income tax rate benefit
